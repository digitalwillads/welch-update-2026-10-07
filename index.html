What was different · 6

The old campaigns did 3 things ours don't

Broad match50% of their keyword spend was broad match, against 11% for ours, so they showed on far more searches.
Ads that ask for a quoteTheir ads said Request a Quote Now. Ours say Free Shipping on $150+ Orders.
Many specific searchesTheir US keywords covered industrial, lab, gauge and piston pumps at $163 a form. Half of ours goes to vacuum pump, at $325 a form.
Not the websiteVisitors who don't come through ads send as many forms as before: about 170 a month in spring, 194 in August and September.
